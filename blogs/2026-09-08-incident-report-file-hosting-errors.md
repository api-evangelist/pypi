Executive summary For about two weeks in August 2026, some PyPI users hit intermittent 502 and 503 errors downloading files from files.pythonhosted.org , triggering failures during installation from PyPI. Thanks to our users filing reports in the support tracker ; one report in particular narrowed the problem to a single cache node. Two separate problems were uncovered.
